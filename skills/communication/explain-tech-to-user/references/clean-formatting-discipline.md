# Mobile-Friendly Formatting Discipline for amirulhazym

Captured from user corrections (2026-08-25):

1. **Avoid Cluttered/Messy ASCII & Dense Multi-level Indents**:
   - On Telegram mobile, nested emoji trees, deep bullet nesting, and complex ASCII art diagrams become visually overwhelming and hard to read ("structure penulisan kau messy").
   - Prefer clean, flat key-value pairs (`- **Key:** Value`) and minimal single-level bullet lists.

2. **Respect "No Analogy" Direct Explanation Mode**:
   - When the user asks for pure/direct explanation with no analogies, explain the technical component, its exact role, and real trade-offs directly without forcing a metaphor.

3. **Separate Strategic Verdict from Supporting Detail**:
   - Deliver clear, direct verdicts first.
   - Group information into logically separated sections with bold headers.
