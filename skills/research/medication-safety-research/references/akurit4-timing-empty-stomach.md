# Akurit-4 (Rifampicin/Isoniazid/Pyrazinamide/Ethambutol) — Timing & Empty Stomach Guidelines

Source: Singapore HealthHub (gov health authority, reviewed Jun 2025)
URLs:
- https://www.healthhub.sg/medication-devices-and-treatment/medications/rifampicin
- https://www.healthhub.sg/medication-devices-and-treatment/medications/isoniazid

## Administration Guidelines

| Drug | Empty Stomach | Timing Consistency | Missed Dose Protocol |
|------|---------------|-------------------|---------------------|
| Rifampicin | "At least 30-60 minutes before food, on an empty stomach. However, if you are having stomach discomfort, you can take them after food instead." | "Take your medications at the same time every day to maintain the same amount of medication in your body." | "If you forget to take a dose, take it as soon as you remember. Then take your next dose at the usual time. Do not take two doses to make up for the missed dose." |
| Isoniazid | "At least 30-60 minutes before food, on an empty stomach." | "Take your medications at the same time every day to maintain the same amount of medication in your body." | "If you forget to take a dose, take it as soon as you remember. Then take your next dose at the usual time. Do not take two doses to make up for the missed dose." |

Both drugs have the same protocol. Since Akurit-4 is a fixed-dose combination, these apply equally.

## Clinical Significance

- Food reduces rifampicin absorption by up to ~30%. Empty stomach requirement is non-negotiable for optimal absorption.
- A single one-off shift of 2-3 hours in timing is NOT clinically significant for TB treatment outcomes.
- Long-term adherence (daily consistency) > perfect clock-time for individual doses.
- The "take as soon as you remember" rule from the official missed-dose protocol is the closest applicable guideline for irregular-hour dosing scenarios.

## Decision Framework for "Take Now vs Wait"

When user is awake at an irregular hour and considering whether to take their daily Akurit-4 dose:

**Take now IF:**
- 2+ hours since last food/drink (other than plain water)
- User is awake and alert — no risk of forgetting or double-dosing
- User can reliably note the time
- Next dose tomorrow can be taken at the usual time (~24-26h gap is acceptable for daily TB meds)

**Wait until normal time IF:**
- User ate within the last 2 hours (food significantly reduces absorption)
- User plans to wake up within 2-3 hours (minimizes schedule disruption)
- User will reliably take it on empty stomach upon waking (won't eat breakfast first)

**Verdict:** Taking now (when awake with empty stomach) is generally better than risking a delayed/late dose or one taken with food interference. The "take as soon as you remember" principle supports this.
