**Source:** DailyMed — Dexamethasone 1.5 mg tablet (Larken Laboratories, Inc.)

## Drug Interactions Listed in FDA Label

The following drugs/substance classes appear in the Drug Interactions section of the FDA label:

- Aminoglutethimide
- Amphotericin B injection (and potassium-depleting agents)
- Antibiotics (macrolides)
- Anticholinesterases
- Oral anticoagulants (warfarin)
- Antidiabetics
- Antitubercular drugs (isoniazid)
- Cholestyramine
- Cyclosporine
- Digitalis glycosides
- Ephedrine
- Estrogens (including oral contraceptives)
- CYP 3A4 inducers/inhibitors/substrates (barbiturates, phenytoin, carbamazepine, rifampin, ketoconazole, erythromycin)
- Ketoconazole
- NSAIDs (aspirin)
- Phenytoin
- Skin tests (corticosteroids suppress reactions)
- Thalidomide
- Vaccines (diminished response)

## What the Label DOES Say About Calcium

The label mentions calcium only in the context of **corticosteroid effects on bone** (under PRECAUTIONS):

> "Corticosteroids decrease bone formation and increase bone resorption both through their effect on calcium regulation (i.e., decreasing absorption and increasing excretion) and inhibition of osteoblast function."

This is a one-way effect: **dexamethasone reduces calcium absorption**, not the reverse. This is why calcium supplementation is standard for patients on long-term corticosteroid therapy — it counters steroid-induced bone loss, not because calcium interacts with the drug.

## Practical Takeaway

- **No evidence calcium carbonate reduces dexamethasone absorption**
- Taking dexamethasone + calcium carbonate + calcitriol together after a meal is fine — no known interaction
- The steroid reduces the body's calcium absorption, so supplementation is actually beneficial
- The 2-4 hour gap recommendation for calcium from other drugs applies to: tetracyclines, levothyroxine, iron, bisphosphonates, quinolone antibiotics — NOT corticosteroids

## Known Drug Interaction Mechanisms for Dexamethasone

- **CYP 3A4 induction:** Dexamethasone is a moderate inducer of CYP 3A4. It can increase clearance of drugs metabolised by this enzyme (e.g., indinavir, erythromycin).
- **CYP 3A4 inhibition by other drugs:** Ketoconazole, macrolide antibiotics can increase dexamethasone plasma concentrations.
- **Potassium depletion:** When combined with potassium-depleting agents (amphotericin B, diuretics), monitor for hypokalemia.
- **Immunosuppression:** Increases infection risk; diminishes vaccine response.
