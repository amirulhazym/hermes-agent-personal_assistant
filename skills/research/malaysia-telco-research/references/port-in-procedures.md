# Port-In / MNP Procedures (Malaysia, 2026)

## CelcomDigi Prepaid Port-In (from any provider)

Source: CelcomDigi official "Switch to CelcomDigi" page (www.celcomdigi.com/switch), extracted July 2026.

### Pre-requisites (Step 01)
1. Line must be **active** — for prepaid, ensure unexpired credit/data
2. No outstanding bills or ongoing contracts with current provider
3. Line must be registered under your own IC. Verify via:
   - Maxis: Send `CHECK<space><601XXXXXXXX>` to 22011
   - U Mobile: Send `Verify<space><ID>` to 28011
   - Yes: (verify via MyYes app or call customer service)

### Choose a plan (Step 02)
Select a plan during signup. Options include:
- Postpaid (with RM60–RM120 port-in rebate depending on plan)
- Prepaid (Hyper 30 RM30, Hyper 35 RM35, Power+ 65 RM65 — all 30-day passes)

### During port-in (Step 03)
1. **Do NOT insert the new CelcomDigi SIM yet.**
2. Wait for a **port-out confirmation SMS** from your current provider. Standard reply formats (case-sensitive):
   - U Mobile: Reply `PortOut Yes 601XXXXXXXX`
   - Maxis: Reply `PortOut Yes 601XXXXXXXX`
3. All supplementary lines must reply accordingly too (if any).
4. Once you lose connection with the old provider, insert the new CelcomDigi SIM.
5. Download the CelcomDigi app to manage your account.

### Timing
- Port takes up to **48 working hours** (Mon–Sun, 8:00 AM – 10:00 PM)
- May take longer if submitted on a public holiday or the day before one
- No service disruption until port successfully completes

### Important notes
- Account auto-terminates with old provider upon successful port — do NOT cancel beforehand
- Port-in rebates (postpaid): RM20/month for 6 months on eligible plans
- For eSIM: you'll receive a QR code via email after sign-up — activate only after port completes

## Yes Prepaid Port-Out

Source: Yes FAQ (enterprisecrm.yes.my/docs/faq), "Switch to Yes" and "General FAQ" categories.

### Process
1. Porting is **initiated by the NEW provider**, not by Yes
2. Do NOT cancel Yes — it auto-terminates on successful port
3. Yes sends a confirmation SMS — reply as instructed
4. Account must be active (not suspended/terminated/blacklisted)
5. No outstanding balance

### eSIM specific
- If you have a Yes eSIM, after port completes you can delete the Yes eSIM profile from your device
- Back up contacts before switching (recommended by Yes)

## CelcomDigi eSIM (Prepaid)

Source: CelcomDigi official eSIM page (www.celcomdigi.com/esim), extracted July 2026.

- eSIM conversion is **FREE** (normally RM10, currently free)
- Supports postpaid plans (Postpaid 5G and CelcomDigi One)
- Activation: receive QR code via email, scan in SIM Manager settings
- Prepaid eSIM: available through the sign-up flow — select eSIM during purchase
